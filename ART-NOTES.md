# Original artwork

Artwork was made with the built-in image-generation tool. The user's reference guided the interface composition; the game uses newly generated landscape and creature assets.

Final files:

- `public/cosmic-world.png`: floating islands, a ringed planet, and an empty summoning platform.
- `public/cosmic-creatures.png`: six original companions, redesigned with natural fur, gills, leaves, shell, and wings instead of crystal armor.
- `public/animal-creatures.png`: twelve natural animal companions in a 4 by 3 atlas.

Latest space-theme revision: both creature sheets now have subtle constellation markings, indigo-cyan nebula gradients on tail or wing tips, and only a few small crystals at an ear base, shoulder, or tail base. Natural anatomy and almost all fur, feathers, skin, and shell remain visible. No crystal armor was added. The revision used the built-in image-generation tool.

Final refinement prompt for both atlases: preserve exact dimensions, transparency, grid, creature identity, pose, and scale; strengthen the space theme with tiny constellation markings and nebula gradients; add only one small cluster of two or three crystals per animal covering less than 3% of its body; no armor, crowns, giant spikes, scenery, text, or objects outside the creature.

Final prompt briefs:

1. Wide painted anime sci-fi environment: blue-purple nebula, luminous ringed planet, floating mossy islands, distant waterfalls, and empty cyan-lit circular platform. No creatures, text, or interface.
2. Preserve a 3 by 2 creature grid: orange-white fox, pale-blue axolotl, green frog, violet jellyfish, golden armadillo, and purple small dragon. Remove all crystal armor and gemstones. Emphasize fur, skin, natural shell, and soft wings with small glowing star markings. Transparent background.
3. Preserve a 4 by 3 grid of wolf, rabbit, bear, owl, tiger, panther, puppy, lion, deer, hedgehog, penguin, and manta ray. Remove crystals, armor, jewelry, and floating orbs. Use natural fur, feathers, quills, fins, and antlers, with restrained glowing markings and tail tips. Transparent background, no scenery or text.
